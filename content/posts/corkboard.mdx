# Project Showcase: Corkboard

> **Status:** MVP in Development  
> **Target:** Hamilton, Ontario Live Music Scene  

Breaking into a city’s live music scene shouldn't feel like uncovering a well-kept secret. Whether it’s a packed indie gig at a local pub or an underground house show, these performances are the heartbeat of a community. 

**Corkboard** is a mobile-first platform designed to bridge the gap between local artists and the community by aggregating fragmented show data into one clean, actionable feed.

---

## The Vision

The goal is simple: **Make local music discoverable.** Current show info is scattered across Instagram flyers, obscure Facebook events, and physical posters. Corkboard curates this content from community sources and venue calendars, presenting it through a map-based and timeline-based interface.

## The Tech Stack

To ensure a smooth, cross-platform experience with real-time updates, Corkboard leverages a modern, scalable architecture:

| Layer | Technology |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Frontend** | React Native, TailwindCSS |
| **Backend** | Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions) |
| **Mapping** | Google Maps SDK |
| **Search / ML** | OpenAI Embeddings, Pinecone / ChromaDB |
| **Storage** | Firebase Storage / Cloudinary |
| **APIs** | Spotify Web API, Eventbrite, IG Scraping |

---

## Why Hamilton?

Hamilton has a legendary music history, but much of its current magic happens in smaller, non-traditional spaces. Corkboard is built to ensure that no matter how small the stage, the right audience can find it.
